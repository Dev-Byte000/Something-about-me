About Me

I’m Hemendra, a BCA student passionate about coding, video editing, and building creative projects. My journey in technology started with C++ and Java, where I learned to design management systems and solve real-world problems using data structures and algorithms. Over time, I expanded my skills into Python and web development, exploring how to build scalable, user-friendly applications. Beyond programming, I enjoy video editing and graphic design, using tools like CapCut and Photoshop to bring ideas to life visually. I’m driven by curiosity, creativity, and the goal of mastering both technical and artistic skills to create impactful projects. My interests also include discrete mathematics, operating systems, and exploring how theory connects with practical applications.
